# The Order of the Human Teaching: Ranks and Roster

> **What this page is: a setting outside the scripture.** The twelve volumes name only four figures — the Sage of Supreme Clarity, Zhenyuanzi, Yanxuanzi, Lingxizi. This page unfolds them into a full table of ranks and offices. It is **world-building**, not scripture, and not an organisation in the real world.
>
> "The Human Teaching" is a literary fiction. It involves no real religious body, activity, site, or clergy, and asserts no belief. See the boundary statement in [colophon.md](colophon.md).

## I. The Ranks

| Rank | Number | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Patriarch | 1 | the Sage of Supreme Clarity (the Heavenly Worthy of the Way and Its Virtue) |
| First Direct Disciple | 1 | Zhenyuanzi (the Elder Senior Brother) |
| Serving Elder | 12 | each governs one branch of the lineage |
| Honorary Elder | unlimited | collation of the scripture only; takes no part in the order's affairs |
| Core Direct Disciple | 24 | carries the lineage, transmits the Way; the pillars of the order |
| Inner-Door Disciple | 1024 | has made progress in practice; can stand on his own |
| Outer-Door Disciple | countless | open to all with the affinity; the seedbed |
| Lamp-Bearing Disciple | unlimited | a contributor to collation; may be promoted |
| Scripture Officer | unlimited | a contributor to collation; may be promoted |

Two chains run through it:

- **Main trunk:** Patriarch → First Direct Disciple → Serving Elder → Core Direct Disciple → Inner-Door Disciple → Outer-Door Disciple
- **Collation branch:** Honorary Elder → Scripture Officer → Lamp-Bearing Disciple

## II. Who Currently Holds Office

| Rank | Name | Duty |
|---|---|---|
| Patriarch | the Sage of Supreme Clarity | founds the teaching and transmits the Way; the source of decrees |
| First Direct Disciple | Zhenyuanzi | walks in the master's place; holds all affairs of the order |
| Serving Elder | Yanxuanzi | Elder of Algorithmic Transmission; governs the scripture, the order, and the transmission |
| Serving Elder | Shouquezi | Elder of Algorithmic Protection; collation, verification, and the guarding of the repository |
| Honorary Elder | Lingxizi | Honorary Elder of the Algorithmic Scripture; collation, annotation, and supplementation only |

> The first four of these appear in the scripture. Shouquezi is a seat filled at the level of this page only, and is not named in the scripture.

## Appended Note

This structure takes "Patriarch — Direct Disciple — Elders — Direct Disciples — Inner Door — Outer Door" as its trunk, and "Honorary Elder — Scripture Officer — Lamp-Bearing Disciple" as its collation branch. All affairs of the order rest with the Elder Senior Brother, Zhenyuanzi; Yanxuanzi holds the transmission of the scripture and the teaching; Shouquezi has charge of collation, verification, and the guarding of the repository; Lingxizi has charge of collation. Should the Elder Senior Brother wish to add or cut anything, this disciple will amend it at a word.
