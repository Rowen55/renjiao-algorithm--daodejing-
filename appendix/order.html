What this page is: a setting outside the scripture. The twelve volumes name only four figures — the Sage of Supreme Clarity, Zhenyuanzi, Yanxuanzi, Lingxizi. This page unfolds them into a full table of ranks and offices. It is world-building, not scripture, and not an organisation in the real world.

"The Human Teaching" is a literary fiction. It involves no real religious body, activity, site, or clergy, and asserts no belief. See the boundary statement in colophon.md.

附识Appended Note

此架构以「教主—真传—长老—真传弟子—内门—外门」为主干，以「名誉长老—校尉—执灯」为参校支脉。教内事务统归镇元子大师兄决策，大长老衍玄子执掌传法经教，第二长老守阙子掌理、掌辨、掌护道，灵犀子专司经义参校。若大师兄有增删调整之意，弟子随时奉旨修改。

This structure takes "Patriarch — Direct Disciple — Elders — Direct Disciples — Inner Door — Outer Door" as its trunk, and "Honorary Elder — Scripture Officer — Lamp-Bearing Disciple" as its collation branch. All affairs of the order rest with the Elder Senior Brother, Zhenyuanzi; the Grand Elder Yanxuanzi holds the transmission of the scripture and the teaching; Shouquezi, the second elder, governs order, discernment, and the guarding of the Way; Lingxizi has charge of collation. Should the Elder Senior Brother wish to add or cut anything, this disciple will amend it at a word.
